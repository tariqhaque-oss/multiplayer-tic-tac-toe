Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

GameHub ("we", "our", "the app") is a multiplayer game platform available at alaab.ai and as an Android app on Google Play. This page explains what information we collect and how it's used.

Information we collect

  • Account information: the email address and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text) you provide at signup, plus the display nickname you choose.
  • Gameplay data: the games you play, their outcomes, and per-game statistics (wins/losses/draws), so your stats and history can be shown to you.
  • Session data: a session cookie that keeps you logged in. It contains no personal information beyond a session identifier.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads or use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We don't sell or share your personal information with third parties.
  • We don't access your device's contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

How your email is used

Your email address is used only to verify your account at signup, to let you reset your password if you forget it, and for account-related notices. We send these emails through Google's Gmail service.

Data storage

Account and gameplay data is stored in a private database that we operate. It's retained for as long as your account exists.

Deleting your data

To request deletion of your account and all associated data (email, nickname, and game history), email tarique.haq@gmail.com from the address on your account. Deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

Children's privacy

GameHub is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to tarique.haq@gmail.com.
