Full description (max 4000 chars)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
GameHub is a real-time multiplayer game hub. Create a free account and play:

- Tic Tac Toe - classic 3x3, random opponent, private games with friends, or bots
- Connect Four - 7x6 gravity board, same random/private/bot matchmaking
- Ludo - 2-4 players race 4 tokens around the board, dice rolls, captures, home stretches
- Court Piece - 4-player trick-taking card game with fixed partnerships and a "Double Sar" rule, fog-of-war enforced so you only ever see your own hand

Every game supports:
- Random matchmaking against other online players
- Private games you create and share a short code for, to play with friends
- Bot opponents when no one else is around (three difficulty levels for the board games)
- Persistent stats - wins, losses, and draws tracked per game

No ads. No third-party trackers. Just the games.
